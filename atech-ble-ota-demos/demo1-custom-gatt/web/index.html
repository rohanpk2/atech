Open 192.168.4.1

This dashboard does not fetch or upload to the device. Disconnect any active Bluetooth session, join the board's hotspot, and finish the upload on its local page.

  1. With the firmware running, hold the physical BOOT button for about 3 seconds, then release it. Do not press RST.
  2. Join Atech-OTA-D1-<suffix> in Wi-Fi settings. The suffix, WPA2 password, and portal token come from the 115200-baud serial output or the deployment record. Stay connected if the phone warns that the network has no Internet.
  3. Open http://192.168.4.1 in Safari or Chrome and enter the portal token.
  4. Select the application firmware.bin, not a bootloader, partitions, or merged image, then tap Deploy Firmware.
  5. Wait for verified success and reboot. Rejoin the hotspot if prompted to see the new version. Wi-Fi mode times out and Bluetooth resumes.
