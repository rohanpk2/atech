    How to connect a new board

    Do this once per board. After it joins your Wi-Fi, every update is wireless, from any browser.

    1. Install the base firmware over USB from the USB install page, then power the board.
    2. In desktop Chrome or Edge, click Connect Bluetooth above and pick the board.
    3. In Wi-Fi setup, click Scan, choose your 2.4 GHz network, enter its password and click Join Wi-Fi. The password goes to the board over an encrypted Bluetooth session and nowhere else. The board is claimed for you automatically.
    4. Once the board shows as online, it stays reachable through the cloud from any browser, including Safari and your phone.

    No Bluetooth? The board has a hotspot page as a backup: hold the physical BOOT button for about 3 seconds, then release it (do not press RST); join the Wi-Fi network Atech-OTA-D1-<suffix> (the suffix is the board's pairing code; the hotspot password and the OTA authorization code are printed on the serial console at boot); open http://192.168.4.1, enter the OTA authorization code, tap Scan, choose your network and tap Join this network. On a local backend, click Find devices afterwards and pair the board with the same code.

    No Wi-Fi or Bluetooth path at all? Download the firmware in step 3, open the board's hotspot page the same way, choose that firmware.bin and tap Deploy Firmware. This dashboard does not fetch or upload over the hotspot. Disconnect any active Bluetooth session first.
