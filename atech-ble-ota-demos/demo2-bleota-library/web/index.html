    How to connect a new board

    Do this once per board. After it joins your Wi-Fi, every update is wireless, from any browser.

    1. Install the base firmware over USB from the USB install page, then power the board.
    2. In desktop Chrome or Edge, click Connect Bluetooth above and pick the board.
    3. In Wi-Fi setup, click Scan, choose your 2.4 GHz network, enter its password and click Join Wi-Fi. The password goes to the board over an encrypted Bluetooth session and nowhere else. The board is claimed for you automatically.
    4. Once the board shows as online, it stays reachable through the cloud from any browser, including Safari and your phone.

    No Bluetooth? The board has its own hotspot page. It comes up by itself when the board has no Wi-Fi yet or cannot reach the cloud; otherwise hold the physical BOOT button for about 3 seconds, then release it (do not press RST). Join the Wi-Fi network ATECH-<suffix> (the suffix is the board's pairing code; the hotspot password and the OTA authorization code are printed on the serial console at boot), open http://192.168.4.1 (or http://atech-<suffix>.local/), enter the OTA authorization code, tap Scan, choose your network and tap Join this network. Enterprise networks (PEAP, TTLS, EAP-TLS) take their CA certificate there too. On a local backend, click Find devices afterwards and pair the board with the same code.

    Board stuck offline, or no Wi-Fi at all? Use the Local Wi-Fi update in step 3: prepare the signed package here, join the board's hotspot and upload it on the board's page. This dashboard does not upload over the hotspot; the board's own page does, and the board verifies the signature before installing. Bluetooth never carries firmware.

    Bluetooth transport
    
          
    Firmware travels with the gb88 BLEOTA 1.0.6 protocol (20-byte START, 4 KB sectors with CRC-16 acknowledgments, then STOP). gb88's own page at gb88.github.io/BLEOTA still works with this firmware.
    
          
    Firmware signing stays disabled, as upstream ships it. The library is AGPL-3.0; review licensing before product use.
